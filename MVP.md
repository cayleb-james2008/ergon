## 1. Target Deployment Profile

A **~25-person regional field-services and installation company**: **project managers (PMs)**, **account executives (AEs)**, **office/admin staff**, and **field installers**.
- **Devices:** Windows/Linux laptops in the office and phones/tablets in the field (authenticated HTTPS `/phone` responsive web view).
- **Operating model:** Non-technical staff run recurring admin, finance, contract, and reporting workflows with mandatory human approval before deletions or external sends; one IT/ops owner deploys, updates, backs up, and supports the server.

## 4. Explicitly Out of Scope (Does Not Block First Deploy)

- Native iOS/Android apps (authenticated HTTPS `/phone` suffices for field installers)
- Multi-region HA clustering or external SQL/S3 primary database migration (one backed-up server fits 25 seats)
- Enterprise SAML/SCIM, SOC 2 Type II, or HIPAA packs (email/password or Google/M365 OIDC + RBAC suffices)
- Deep bi-directional ERP/dispatch sync (QuickBooks, ServiceTitan, Procore) beyond CSV, webhooks, and email
- Third-party plugin marketplace or cross-company skill sharing
- Per-laptop local GPU hosting, standalone `ergon-web.html` parity, or out-of-repo companion prototypes
- Unattended outbound email/invoice sending (human approval stays mandatory for v1)
