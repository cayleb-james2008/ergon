Rules these states follow

  • Plain words — no "diagnostics", "enrolled", "payload", "unconfirmed" on employee screens.
  • One next step — every state names the one thing to do next; Back/Dismiss stay secondary.
  • No dead ends — errors say what happened, that nothing was lost, and what happens next.
  • No loop tells — loading is a static skeleton with a worded label; no pulse, ping, shimmer or spinner loop.
  • Reduced motion / High Contrast — nothing in these states depends on motion or colour alone.
