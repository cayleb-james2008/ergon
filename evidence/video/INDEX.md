### B. Real Product Defects Caught Uncut During Video Proof

1. **`FAIL` — Background scheduler skips deterministic zero-LLM routines when no model is configured (`ergon-routines`, `00:27`):** `src/server.rs:1029` (`start_scheduler_thread`) skips ALL scheduled ticks whenever `!eff.model_configured()`, even for deterministic `steps_v2` routines that require zero LLM calls, while the UI displays `Scheduler active (2s tick)` and leaves the routine overdue (`1 missed`).
2. **`FAIL` — Routine catch-up double-records runs and flags false `Drift` (`ergon-routines`, `00:29`):** A single manual `Catch up` invokes `routines::record_run` twice (`src/pipelines.rs:2297` inside `execute_deterministic_steps_sync` and again at `src/server.rs:531` in `execute_routine_run`), inflating `run_count` to `2` and displaying a false `Drift` pill.
3. **`PARTIAL` — `FileViewerModal` renders `Save as PDF` enabled on `.csv` files (`ergon-connectors-studio`, `00:13`):** `frontend/src/shell/WorkspaceFiles.tsx:1379` leaves `#file-save-pdf` active for `.csv` files, triggering `HTTP 400 Bad Request` from `src/tools.rs:1365` (`"export_file: PDF export takes a .md or .txt file"`).
