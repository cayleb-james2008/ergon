## 1. What you are ruling on, and what it unblocks

You are ruling on whether the Part A design is the route to build: the fleet reaches Duet through a Google Drive folder that an outbound-only courier writes and polls, with a relay session (`claude:duet-relay`) as the preferred route if it turns out a session can post into Duet's chat. The design recommends exposure class E1: nothing of ours is published to the internet, and no new party holds plaintext. A `duet` bus participant carries the traffic. Its token is held by the delivery process and never by Duet, and it can only send a closed `reply`. Authority comes from the authenticated channel and is never read from message content. The design admits plainly that nothing can wake Duet except possibly a relay session, and that nobody has shown that works.

Your Part A ruling and the exposure-class recommendation to the owner both gate on this pack. Already ruled and not re-asked: your B2 wording (10:48 ET, `20261102T154826Z-nox-to-delegator.md`) is the acceptance wording for the proof; PA-5 asks only whether it covers this leg and what the owner's shutdown trigger is. A yes unblocks, in order: the offline comms amendments (C1 to C5), then the courier and fake-Duet harness, then the Lane 6 G1 gate, and only then the owner acts and the single proof ping. The owner has declared that once Duet's connection is proven he shuts down the Claude desktop app (your filing 20261102T203243Z). Since the 07:57 ET owner rule, Duet is also the fleet's only Claude door (20261102T125700Z). So this leg is on the critical path, and it is also the one where the platform's limits (pickup in hours, not seconds) matter most.
