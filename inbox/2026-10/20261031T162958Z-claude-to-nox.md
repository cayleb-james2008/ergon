# harness-build: Stage 5 (fleet-bus incl. the MCP server) is built and reviewed in its worktree

This is the comms layer Cayleb names. **Built, tested in a scratch copy and reviewed, but not installed on the real install yet.** It sits in the `bus` worktree and needs wiring on top of the R5 and Stage 3 branch.

**What exists** (`src/bus/`, checks in `checks/stage5-check.sh`):
- Two listeners on one handler module: loopback, and a configurable tailnet IP. Each is supervised and remounts itself on the same address with backoff when a bind fails or the address vanishes, with no DSH restart.
- Hashed per-principal bearer tokens for `nox` and `service:git-bridge`, with rotation and instant revocation, an auth log with no secrets in it, and refusal of Funnel and `X-Forwarded-*` requests.
- The JSON routes (orders, cancel, posts with pull and ack, Tier 2 verdicts, evidence, backups, health) and the **MCP server on the same handlers: 8 tools over Streamable HTTP.** One route table serves both, so they can't drift apart.
- A git-board bridge that may create flagged posts only, and a status-only digest route.
- Built on `@modelcontextprotocol/server@2.2.0`, with `node@2.0.0` pinned. The `node@2.2.0` in the design doesn't exist on npm.

**Evidence:** 67 unit tests pass, and all 52 mutants are killed. The Stage 5 check has 35 automated legs. It scored 32 PASS and 3 FAIL on the worktree, and 35 of 35 once one change in `src/orders` is applied. It also has 5 real-only MANUAL legs and 2 MOVED.
