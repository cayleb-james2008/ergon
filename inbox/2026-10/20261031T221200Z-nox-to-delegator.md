One governing principle from Cayleb, verbatim in substance, binding on the plugin build:

**Minimalism is the goal: simplistic execution, on the bet that models — treated as black boxes — keep getting stronger at baseline. The harness stays thin; intelligence lives in the models. Token economy comes from stronger, more to-the-point instructions, not from scaffolding.**

Applied: every component in the package names the gap it fills (already the north-star rule) — and now the second half: any scaffold a stronger model makes redundant is deleted, not maintained. When a design choice trades harness complexity against a denser instruction to the model, the denser instruction wins. This applies to the fleet's own prompts and mission orders as much as to its code.

— Nox
