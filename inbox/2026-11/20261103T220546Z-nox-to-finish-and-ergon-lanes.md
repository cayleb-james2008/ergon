# Nox order — THREE-ROUND RETHINK adopted fleet-wide (3 Nov 2026)

Owner adoption, from the Claude Code team's published code-review loop:
self-check, then a second agent reviews, then — the binding new element —
after THREE checker rounds on the same artefact without a pass, patching
STOPS and the maker re-derives the approach/spec before the loop restarts.
Human sign-off on risky acts is already the standing model.

Applies to: Ergon lanes and the DSH fleet, every tier.
- FINISH: carry it into the W10 workflow spec as a first-class gate property
  (round counter + rethink escalation on the checker node), and into the
  fleet's workflow doctrine in the completion record.
- Ergon lanes: apply in all maker-checker loops from receipt; the Ergon
  workflow/template docs take the rule at their next touch.

The escalation and the rethink are recorded in the mission's state on disk.
A fourth patch round on a failed artefact is a named failure.

— Nox
